### Fixed

- **Time counted while the games were waiting.** When a game of the account is open on another device (the Steam client), SteamEdge holds its own games; the statistics, the hour sync ledger, the timed hour boost and the card farming clocks used to keep running anyway. They now stand still while the games wait and go on from the same point when the other game closes, the way they already did for a dropped connection.

### Added

- **Card farming estimate, as a notification.** After the third card of a run, and then every twentieth, a notification tells how many cards are left and the estimated finish, worked out from the pace of that run (the warm-up counts, so the first estimate is on the long side). Nothing was added to the pages.
- **Connection summary on the overview.** Once after the app opens, the recent activity shows the drops of the last 24 hours. Nothing is added when there were none.

### Changed

- **Five notification sounds instead of 23:** Hit, Door knock, Dull thud, Drip and Whoosh, of different lengths and none of them a melody, plus Silent. A saved choice that is gone becomes Door knock.
- **"Compact rows" is gone:** it did what "Interface density" already does. A saved "compact rows" turns the density into Compact.
- **Shorter setting descriptions** for the 14 longest ones (proxies, reconnecting, log file, bulk selling, schedules, session timeout, resuming jobs, unlock interval, GPU compositing, start in tray, card drop threshold, Family View PIN, average price), in all six languages.
- `npm test` runs 67 tests; `farm-hold` and a new boost scenario check the held clocks.
