The main process and the Steam engine are split into more files. Nothing changes for you except one known problem, below.

### Changed

- **`main.js` went from 1015 to 479 lines.** Two more parts moved into their own files under `src/backend/`, unchanged: the account switcher with connecting and the proxy of the account on screen (`accounts.js`), and the lists, library, inventory, achievements, profile, sale and market listing handlers (`inventory.js`).
- **`steamEngine.js` went from 1744 to 787 lines.** The class methods moved into `src/core/engine/`, unchanged: the reconnect loop (`connection.js`), prices, order book and listings (`market.js`), achievements and stats (`achievements.js`), keys, free apps, booster packs and the parental PIN (`store.js`) and chat (`chat.js`). All 72 methods are still on the class.

### Known issues

- **Selling items fails with "accountStats is not defined".** A name that did not follow the 1.9.5 move. It is fixed in 1.9.7.
