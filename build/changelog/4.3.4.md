Two themes, and a card farm that really stands still while you play.

### Changed

- **Only two themes: VantaBlack and VantaWhite.** The coloured themes (R34 Purple, Crimson, Olive, Indigo) are gone. The two that are left take their colours and names from the schemes of the same names in the Vantagraph theme: VantaBlack is the neutral near-black with the light grey accent, VantaWhite is the paper white with the near-black accent. A copy that had one of the removed themes saved opens in VantaBlack. "Automatic" is VantaBlack.

### Fixed

- **The card farm kept running while you played the same account yourself.** When you start a game from the Steam client on the same account, Steam counts nothing for the farm, and the farm is meant to wait. Only the other modes did: Fast mode kept its rotation and its clock going, so the page looked busy and the time passed. Now Fast mode stops its rotation too, the clock stands still and the page says it is paused; when your game ends it goes on from where it was. The elapsed time of the other modes is frozen the same way.
- **A single game in Fast mode** is checked every 80 seconds now (it is sent to Steam once and stays open; nothing is sent again until the list of games changes).

### Notes

- Only the account that stays online although it was set to Invisible is still open. `logs/events.log` (4.3.3) shows what is sent and what Steam answers.

**As always:** never share your `settings/` folder - it holds your Steam login token.
