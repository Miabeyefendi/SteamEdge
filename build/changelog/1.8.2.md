Playing a game in the Steam client no longer starts a tug of war with SteamEdge, finished games leave the Realistic Mode queue, and games without achievements are not offered any more.

### Fixed

- **Opening a game in the Steam client while SteamEdge ran jobs on the same account started a logon every six seconds.** Steam lets one session of an account play. When the Steam client starts a game it takes that from SteamEdge (LoggedInElsewhere); SteamEdge took it for a dropped connection, logged on again five seconds later, opened its games and was thrown out again, for as long as you played (seen on a real account; in a test of ten minutes, 107 logons). Now the connection comes back once and SteamEdge opens no game while one of the account is open elsewhere; a strip under the top bar says so. When that game closes, Steam says so and the jobs' games open again at once. If Steam says nothing, SteamEdge tries again after two minutes, not after six seconds. Realistic Mode waits meanwhile, the unlocks are not counted as failed.
- **Games with every achievement unlocked stayed in the Realistic Mode queue.** A run skipped them at every start and they never left. A game now leaves the saved queue when a run unlocks its last achievement, when a run starts and finds nothing left in it, or when the plan finds it done. The feed says so. A game whose target was only a part of its achievements stays. Its panel said "No achievements in this game" for a game with all of them unlocked (2130 on Zup!); it says "Nothing left to unlock" now.

### Changed

- **The game list of Realistic Mode only offers games with achievements.** The switch "Show games without achievements" is gone: such a game could only be added to be thrown out again. A game Steam marks wrongly is still dropped as soon as its achievements are read, as before.
- `npm test` runs 66 tests. New: `playing-elsewhere` (a game on another device for ten minutes, a silent Steam, a plain drop) and `realistic-finished` (one after the other, together, a partial target).

**As always:** never share your `settings/` folder - it holds your Steam login token.
